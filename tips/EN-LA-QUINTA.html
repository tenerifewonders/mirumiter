LA QUINTA (SANTA ÚRSULA) - FOLLOWING THE MENCEY'S TRAIL. GUANCHE LEGENDS
This scenic walking route through La Quinta (Santa Úrsula) follows coastal viewpoints and historical
landmarks connected to Guanche heritage.
ROUTE TYPE: Self-guided walking route.
ESTIMATED DURATION: ~1 hour 30 minutes
DISTANCE: Approx. 3.5 km
ROUTE OVERVIEW (STOPS):
1. La Giganta (La Quinta Park)
2. Barranco de la Cruz
3. Mirador Callado Blanco
4. Búnker de Santa Úrsula
5. Mirador de La Quinta Roja
VISITABLE PLACES & OPENING HOURS:

• La Giganta & La Quinta Park: public outdoor space, open daily.
• Miradores (Callado Blanco, La Quinta Roja): open-access viewpoints, available at all times.
• Búnker: exterior visit.
PRACTICAL TIPS FOR THE ROUTE
• Footwear: Wear comfortable walking shoes—some paths include slopes and uneven
terrain.
• Weather & sun: Bring water, sunscreen, and a hat—many sections are exposed.
• Pace: Allow time to stop and enjoy viewpoints—this route is as much about scenery as
walking.
• Respect the environment: These are natural and residential areas—avoid littering and keep
noise low.
This route offers a unique combination of landscape, history, and legend, guiding visitors through
one of Tenerife’s most evocative coastal settings while retracing echoes of the island’s ancient
Guanche past.
